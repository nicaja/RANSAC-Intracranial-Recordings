Strengths & Weaknesses of RANSAC applied to Epidural & Intracortical Recordings

Nickolaj Ajay Atchuthan, Felipe Rettore Andreis, Winnie Jensen & Suzan Meijs
IEEE EMBC 2025 · paper · code

RANSAC halved the variance of our epidural recording and raised its correlation from 0.85 to 1.00. A noise-only recording also went from 0.86 to 1.00. On the intracortical array RANSAC rejected half the contacts and rebuilt most of them with larger peaks that line up in time.
