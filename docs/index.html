Consensus on the array

RANSAC flags channels it cannot predict from the rest of the array and rebuilds them by interpolation. On a surface µECoG grid that removed faulty channels. On a penetrating array it reshaped real responses. On pure noise it produced a convincing, highly correlated signal.

From Strengths & Weaknesses of RANSAC applied to Epidural & Intracortical Recordings, Atchuthan, Andreis, Jensen & Meijs · code and figures

Fig. 1

Every electrode, in place

Each cell is one channel's average over all stimuli, on one shared voltage scale. Flagged channels fade into their rebuilt signal; good channels did not change. The band underneath is the spread across all channels, with good channels alone in grey. Hover to read values at any time point.

Fig. 2

How the verdict builds up

For every epoch, RANSAC predicts each channel from random subsets of the others and flags it when the prediction correlates poorly. A channel flagged in more than 40% of epochs is interpolated everywhere.
